Compression Tips

  • Low (10-30%): Best for thumbnails and web graphics
  • Medium (40-60%): Good balance for most web images
  • High (70-90%): Recommended for photos and prints
  • JPG images compress better than PNG for photos
  • PNG is better for images with transparency
