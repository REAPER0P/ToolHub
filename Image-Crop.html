Image Crop Tool

Crop images precisely by selecting the exact area you want. Supports free crop, fixed ratios, and circle cropping.

How to Use

  • Upload an image from your device
  • Select crop type or aspect ratio
  • Adjust crop area and download image

Features

  • Free & fixed aspect ratio cropping
  • Circle crop support
  • Zoom & pan controls
  • No server upload – 100% browser based
