How to Use

  • Upload: Click the upload area or drag & drop your image
  • Select Shape: Choose rectangle, circle, rounded corners, or free form
  • Adjust Crop: Drag the image to position, resize as needed
  • Set Ratio: Choose aspect ratio or keep free form
  • Apply: Click "Apply Crop & Download" to process
  • Download: Get your cropped image instantly
  • Reset: Use "Reset" button to start over

Features

  • Multiple crop shapes: Rectangle, Circle, Rounded corners, Free form
  • Adjustable corner radius for rounded crops
  • 10+ aspect ratio presets (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, etc.)
  • Real-time crop dimensions display
  • Drag to reposition image
  • Instant preview before download
  • Drag & drop file upload
  • 100% browser-based - no server upload
  • Maintains image quality
  • Mobile responsive design
  • Secure processing - files never leave your device

Crop Types

  • Rectangle: Standard rectangular crop with sharp corners
  • Circle: Perfect circular crop for profile pictures
  • Rounded Corners: Rectangle with adjustable corner radius
  • Free Form: Custom crop without constraints
  • Aspect Ratio: Maintain specific width:height ratio
  • Social Media: Pre-set ratios for Instagram, Facebook, etc.
