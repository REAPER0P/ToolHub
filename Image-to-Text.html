How to Use for Best Results

  • Use clear images: Ensure text is clearly visible and not blurry
  • Good lighting: Avoid shadows on the text
  • High contrast: Dark text on light background works best
  • Straight alignment: Keep text horizontally aligned
  • File formats: Use PNG, JPG, or JPEG for best results
